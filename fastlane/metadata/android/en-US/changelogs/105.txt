Release candidate (0.13.1): inventory, for those who want it. Turn it on for your Pantry or any tab you made and each item shows how much you have at home: a number, a weight or volume, or roughly (a quarter, half or three quarters of a pack). Ticking something off while shopping adds it, and Restock can ask or add it to your list when you run low. Off unless you turn it on. Also: Restore from a backup on the first screen is a plain tap on the file picker.
