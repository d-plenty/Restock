Release candidate 3. Restock server: sync through a small server you run yourself (one docker run command, github.com/d-plenty/restock-server), end-to-end encrypted. Each phone joins with a one-time code and its own key. Older prices and receipt photos move to the server, only once it has them. Use Restock on your computer in a browser: sign in with your passphrase or by scanning a code. Restock still works without it.
