Release candidate (0.14.0): Restock now runs on iPhone and iPad too, and an Android phone can sync with one over your Wi-Fi. Export data can send a copy straight to another phone with a QR code; Import data takes a file or a code, and adds alongside what's there or replaces it. Loyalty cards can have an expiry date, with a reminder a month before. A Restock server is no longer the recommended way to sync: phone sharing is.
