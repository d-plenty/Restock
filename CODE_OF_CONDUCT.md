# Code of Conduct

Restock is for everyone who does the shopping, so this space is too.

**Be kind and patient.** People here are volunteers, beginners and experts alike. Assume good intent, explain things plainly, and help people get there.

**Keep it on topic.** Talk about the app, its code and making shopping fairer. No politics, no selling, no spam.

**Respect privacy.** Never post someone else's personal details, and don't ask people for theirs.

**Not okay:** harassment, insults, discrimination of any kind, sexual content, threats, or deliberately stirring people up.

**What happens:** the maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who don't follow this, in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions. To report a problem privately, use the **Report content** option on any comment, or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Thanks for helping keep it a good place.
